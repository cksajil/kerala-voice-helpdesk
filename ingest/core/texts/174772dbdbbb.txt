Grievance Redressal
Grievance Redressal Mechanisms in Local Self-Government Institutions
Major Grievance Redressal Mechanisms
⚖️ 1. Local Self-Government Ombudsman
OMBUDSMAN FOR LSGIs
The Ombudsman is a higher-level judicial mechanism for submitting complaints regarding corruption, irregularities, abuse of power, and dereliction of duty by elected representatives or officials of Local Self-Government Institutions (LSGIs). The Ombudsman is headed by a person holding the rank of a High Court Judge.
🏛️ 2. Local Self-Government Institutions Tribunal
LSGI TRIBUNAL
The LSGI Tribunal is a judicial forum for filing appeals against decisions and orders issued by the Panchayat Governing Committee or Secretary, including rejection of licences, cancellation of building construction permits, and other related matters. Its headquarters is in Thiruvananthapuram.
💻 3. Chief Minister’s Portal
CMO PORTAL
Delays or other difficulties encountered in Panchayat services can be brought directly to the attention of the Chief Minister through the online portal. Complaints submitted through cmo.kerala.gov.in are required to be addressed by the concerned officials within the prescribed time limit.
📱 4. K-SMART Portal
K-SMART CITIZEN PORTAL
K-SMART is the online single-window system of the Local Self-Government Department. Citizens can submit complaints related to Panchayat services online without visiting the Panchayat office in person. They can also track the real-time status of their complaints.
🔍 5. Vigilance Department
VACB – Vigilance and Anti-Corruption Bureau
Complaints can be submitted directly to the Vigilance and Anti-Corruption Bureau (VACB) regarding demands for bribes, large-scale financial irregularities, diversion or misuse of government funds, and other corrupt practices that may occur in Panchayat offices.
